How deterioration is flagged

01 Minimum Flow ACH Inceased ≥ 100 ACH

ACH at minimum flow increased by 100 air changes per hour or more.

02 Crosses the 200–400 range

Baseline is 200–400 inclusive and latest ACH is below 200 or above 400.

These are user-defined monitoring rules, not a determination of hood safety or regulatory compliance. Verify any flagged result against applicable facility procedures and qualified personnel.
